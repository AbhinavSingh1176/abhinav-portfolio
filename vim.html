Vibration Isolation Module

A passive isolation stage for a 2.5 kg camera and LiDAR payload on a delivery robot, taken from a MATLAB model to tested hardware.

The target is at least 40% RMS acceleration reduction across the 5–30 Hz band, shown with accelerometer data rather than a simulation alone. This page is the running log, updated as each phase clears.

Specifications

Payload2.5 kg camera + LiDAR package on a last-mile delivery robot
Target≥ 40% RMS acceleration reduction across the 5–30 Hz band
ModelMATLAB SDOF: transmissibility, terrain PSD response, bump response (fn = 3.5 Hz, ζ ≈ 0.12)
StructureParallel four-bar linkage, waterjet 6061 aluminum, ±15 mm stroke, k = 1200 N/m
AnalysisANSYS static (3g, SF ≥ 2) and modal (structural modes above 50 Hz)
ValidationTwo MPU6050 accelerometers on an Arduino DAQ; log-decrement and broadband PSD comparison against the model
